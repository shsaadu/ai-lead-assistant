# Northstar Plumbing: prices and call-outs

Northstar Plumbing is a fictional plumbing company used as a demo.

## Emergency call-outs

- We handle emergency leaks, burst pipes, blockages and no-hot-water problems across South East England.
- Emergency call-out fee inside the M25: a fixed £89. The call-out fee is waived if you go ahead with the repair during the same visit.
- There is no extra charge for evening or weekend emergency call-outs.

## Labour

- The call-out fee covers the first hour on site.
- After the first hour, labour is £75 per hour.
- Parts and materials are charged at cost, and we tell you the price before fitting anything.

## Quotes

- Bathroom and kitchen installations are quoted individually after a free survey visit.
- We never give fixed prices for complex jobs over chat; the team confirms them after seeing the job.
