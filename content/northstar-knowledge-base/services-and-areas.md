# Northstar Plumbing: services and areas

## Services

- Emergency repairs: leaks, burst pipes, blockages and no hot water.
- Bathroom and kitchen plumbing: new fixtures, pipework, appliance connections and complete installations.
- Maintenance and servicing: small repairs and preventative checks.

## Areas covered

- South East England.
- Emergency call-outs are available inside the M25. For addresses outside the M25, the team will confirm availability.

## Availability

- Emergency call-outs: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- Average response time for urgent leaks and loss of water: 38 minutes inside the M25.
- Non-urgent work and surveys: Monday to Saturday, booked with the team.

## What to do in a leak emergency

- Turn off the water at your stopcock (often under the kitchen sink).
- If water is near electrics, keep away and switch off the electricity at the consumer unit only if it's safe to do so.
- Then contact us and we'll send someone as quickly as possible.
