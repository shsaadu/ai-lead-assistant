# Features, languages and limits

## Languages

The assistant replies in the language the customer writes in. The chat window and enquiry form are translated into 12 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Arabic (shown right to left), Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Turkish and Russian.

## Voice messages

Customers can tap the microphone in the chat and speak instead of typing, in any language, including languages that are hard to type, such as Bengali. The assistant writes down what they said, shows it in the chat so they can see it was understood, and answers as normal. If the customer spoke, the reply is also read aloud when their phone or computer has a voice for that language (most have English and the main European and Asian languages; some languages, such as Bengali, aren't available on every device). Recordings can be up to 30 seconds. The recording is only used to write down the message and isn't stored; only the text is saved. Voice messages are included in every plan at no extra cost.

This is voice inside the website chat. The assistant doesn't answer phone calls.

## Accuracy

The assistant is instructed to answer only from the information the business provides. If something isn't covered, it says so and offers to pass the question to the business's team, rather than guessing prices, dates or availability. Businesses can read every conversation in their dashboard.

## What it won't do

- It doesn't give professional advice the business shouldn't give through a chat, such as legal, medical or immigration advice. Those questions are passed to the business's team.
- It can't book appointments, take payments or send someone out by itself. It collects the customer's details so the team can follow up.
- It doesn't answer on WhatsApp yet (in development), but customers can leave a WhatsApp number instead of an email.

## Protection against misuse

The assistant has limits on how many messages one visitor can send, to stop spam and keep the service available.

## Speed

Answers usually arrive within a few seconds.
