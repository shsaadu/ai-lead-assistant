# Assist Street's services

Assist Street's services are grouped into three areas: Answer, Automate and Build. Each is described on the Services page of the website, and demos are on the Demos page.

## Answer: AI assistants

- Website assistant (available now): an AI assistant on the business's website that answers customers from the business's own information, in their language, 24/7, and sends ready-to-act enquiries to the team.
- WhatsApp assistant (coming soon, not available yet): the same assistant answering on the business's WhatsApp Business number, using the official WhatsApp Business Platform.
- Booking assistant (coming soon, not available yet): offers real free slots from the business's calendar, books appointments inside the chat, and sends confirmations.

## Automate: less admin

- Lead follow-up (available now): an instant, personal acknowledgement of every enquiry, polite follow-ups if the customer goes quiet, and reminders to the team so nobody is forgotten.
- Workflow automation (available now): connecting forms, spreadsheets, CRM and email so information flows automatically, built with reliable automation tools and documented for the team.

## Build: bigger projects

- Website design and build (available now): modern, fast, mobile-friendly websites with an AI assistant built in, with hosting, updates and care looked after. The Assist Street website itself is an example.
- Staff knowledge assistant (available now): a private assistant trained on the business's handbooks and procedures that answers staff questions and points to the source. Only the business's own team can use it.

## Getting started

- AI opportunity call (free): a relaxed 30-minute call about how the business works and where AI could genuinely save time, including honest advice when AI isn't worth it.
- Team AI workshop: a practical half-day session, at the client's office or online, using AI tools on the team's real work, with simple guidelines on using AI safely with customer data.
