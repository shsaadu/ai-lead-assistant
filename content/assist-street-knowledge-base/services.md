# Assist Street's services

Assist Street's services come in three levels. Each is described on the Services page of the website, with a demo for each level on the Demos page.

## Level 1: Assistant

An AI assistant on the business's existing website. It answers customers from the business's own information, in their language, 24/7, and sends ready-to-act enquiries to the team. Assist Street sets it up and looks after it. Plans are Starter and Managed. Demo: a plain small-business website where the assistant is added with one line of code.

## Level 2: Website plus assistant

A new hand-built website with the assistant built in from day one: modern, fast and mobile-friendly, with hosting, updates and care looked after. Demo: a language school's website with a multilingual assistant. The Assist Street website itself is another example.

## Level 3: Automation and AI

Built to order around how the business works, quoted after a free call. Examples: instant acknowledgements and polite follow-ups, bookings and reminders, invoice reminders, a plain-English weekly summary, connecting forms, spreadsheets, CRM and email, reading and checking supplier invoices and forms, and a private staff knowledge assistant. Assist Street never handles a client's money: it can remind, but the business is paid directly. These are not off-the-shelf products. Demo: a dental practice, following one patient from enquiry to invoice, with an interactive preview of what the practice sees.

## On the roadmap

A WhatsApp assistant and a booking assistant are in development. They are not available yet.

## Getting started

- Free 30-minute call: a relaxed call about how the business works and where AI could genuinely save time, including honest advice when AI isn't worth it. There is no free trial or pilot.
- Team AI workshop: a practical half-day session, at the client's office or online, using AI tools on the team's real work, with simple guidelines on using AI safely with customer data.

To book a call or a workshop, visitors can leave their details in this chat and the Assist Street team will reply within one working day.
