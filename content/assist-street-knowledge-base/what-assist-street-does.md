# What Assist Street does

Assist Street sets up and looks after AI assistants for UK businesses' websites. The assistant is a chat window on the business's website that:

- answers customers' questions using only the business's own information (prices, services, availability, policies, FAQs)
- replies in the language the customer writes in, at any time of day or night
- notices when a customer is ready to book, wants a quote, or wants to speak to a person, and then asks for their details
- collects the details the business needs, using the business's own questions (for example dates, budget or service needed), with email or a phone/WhatsApp number
- sends each enquiry to the business by email and into a dashboard, with a short English summary and the customer's language

Assist Street does the setup, testing and updates, so the business doesn't need technical skills or staff time.

Alongside the website assistant, Assist Street also builds new websites with the assistant built in, and custom automation and AI built to order, such as follow-ups, reminders, paperwork reading and staff knowledge assistants. It also runs team AI workshops. The way to start is a free 30-minute call. WhatsApp and booking assistants are on the roadmap, not available yet. See the services document for details.

## Who it's for

Any business that gets questions through its website: for example schools and training providers, clinics, trades and home services, property and lettings, and professional services. It's especially useful for businesses whose customers write outside office hours or in other languages.

There are three example businesses with working assistants, all linked from the Demos page of the Assist Street website (/demos). Visitors can try asking questions in any language.

- Northstar Plumbing, a plumbing company: emergency call-outs, prices and areas covered. Link: /demo/northstar/

All three are fictional businesses made for the demos. Lumi, on the Assist Street website itself, is a fourth working example.
