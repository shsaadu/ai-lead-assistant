# Pricing

All prices are starting prices, in pounds. Assist Street agrees a fixed quote with every client before any work starts. Assist Street is not VAT-registered yet, so no VAT is added. Charges from other services (WhatsApp messages, text messages, automation tool subscriptions) are passed on at cost and agreed first.

There is no free trial or free pilot. Every client starts with a free 30-minute call, where Assist Street asks how the business works, says honestly what fits (including when AI is not worth it), and gives a fixed quote. Plans roll monthly with no long contract: a client can cancel with 30 days' notice.

## Founding client offer

The first 10 clients get half-price setup on any assistant plan or website.

## Assistant (an AI assistant on the business's existing website)

- Starter: £79 per month plus £249 one-off setup. Assistant on one website, answers from the business's own information, 12 languages, the business's own questions on the enquiry form, email alerts and a dashboard. Up to 500 conversations a month.
- Managed: £199 per month plus £399 one-off setup. Everything in Starter, plus Assist Street updates the information whenever it changes, reviews unanswered questions every month, sends a monthly report on enquiries, and gives priority support. Up to 1,500 conversations a month.

## Website plus assistant (a new hand-built website with the assistant built in)

- Essential website: from £1,950 plus £99 per month for hosting, care and the assistant. About 5 pages.
- Business website: from £3,500 plus £149 per month. Up to about 10 pages, with enquiry forms, integrations, multilingual pages and Managed assistant features.

## Automation and AI (built to order, quoted after a free call)

These are built to order with proven tools or custom code. They are not off-the-shelf products. The demos on the website show the kind of thing Assist Street builds.

- Single automation: from £750, plus any tool subscriptions at cost. For example enquiry follow-ups, booking reminders or invoice reminders.
- Connected system: from £2,400. Three to five workflows working together.
- Paperwork reading (supplier invoices and forms): quoted, from about £2,500. Scanning photos and PDFs is scoped with the client.
- Staff knowledge assistant: from £1,500 plus £150 per month.
- Care plan: £199 per month for monitoring, fixes and up to two hours of changes to anything Assist Street has built.
