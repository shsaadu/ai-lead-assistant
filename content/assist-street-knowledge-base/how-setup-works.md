# How setup works

Getting an assistant live takes three steps:

1. The business sends its information: brochures, price lists, website pages, FAQs, or anything its team uses to answer customers.
2. Assist Street builds and tests the assistant: the business's colours and name, the questions it needs answered before it can quote, and limits on topics the assistant shouldn't advise on (for example legal, medical or immigration advice). It's tested with real customer questions before going live.
3. The business adds one line of code to its website, or asks whoever looks after its website to do it. It works on most websites and website builders that allow adding a line of code.

Assist Street can also limit the assistant to the business's own website, so nobody else can put it on another site.

Updates: when prices, dates or services change, the business tells Assist Street and the assistant's information is updated. On the Managed plan, Assist Street handles updates whenever needed.
