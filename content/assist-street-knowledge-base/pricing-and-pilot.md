# Pricing and the free pilot

## Free 30-day pilot

Every new client starts with a free 30-day pilot:

- the full assistant on the business's website, set up by Assist Street
- the business's own information and enquiry questions
- email alerts and the dashboard
- at the end, a review of every conversation and enquiry it handled

There's no contract and no card needed for the pilot. If the business doesn't want to continue, the assistant is removed at no charge.

## Plans after the pilot

Essentials: £75 per month, plus a one-off setup fee of £200. Includes the assistant on the website, answers from the business's information, the business's own questions on the enquiry form, email alerts and the dashboard.

Managed: £150 per month, plus a one-off setup fee of £300. Includes everything in Essentials, plus Assist Street updates the information whenever it changes, reviews unanswered questions every month, and sends a monthly report on enquiries.

WhatsApp integration (the assistant answering on a WhatsApp Business number) is in development and not yet available. Businesses interested in trying it early can ask.

For anything not covered here, such as custom integrations, Mohammad will discuss it directly.
