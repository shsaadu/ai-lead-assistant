# Pricing and the free pilot

All prices are starting prices. Assist Street agrees a fixed quote with every client before any work starts. Assist Street is not VAT-registered yet, so no VAT is added. Charges from other services (WhatsApp messages, text messages, automation tool subscriptions) are passed on at cost and agreed first.

## Free 30-day pilot

Every new website assistant starts with a free 30-day pilot: the full assistant on the business's website, set up by Assist Street, with the business's own information and enquiry questions, email alerts and the dashboard. At the end, the business gets a review of every conversation and enquiry. There's no contract and no card needed. If the business doesn't continue, the assistant is removed at no charge.

## Website assistant plans

- Essentials: £75 per month, plus £200 one-off setup. The assistant on the website, answers from the business's information, its own questions on the enquiry form, email alerts and the dashboard.
- Managed: normally £200 per month, plus £400 one-off setup. Everything in Essentials, plus Assist Street updates the information whenever it changes, reviews unanswered questions every month, and sends a monthly report on enquiries.
- Early client offer: the first 10 clients get the Managed plan for £150 per month instead of £200, fixed for their first 12 months.
