# Student life and common questions

## Social programme

- Free activities every week: a London walking tour on Mondays, a conversation club on Wednesdays and a film club on Fridays.
- Paid weekend trips, for example to Oxford, Cambridge, Brighton and Bath. Trips cost from £45 to £75, including transport.
- See the activity board in the student lounge, or ask reception, for this week's programme.

## Common questions

**What age do I need to be?**
16 or over. Students aged 16 or 17 must stay in homestay.

**Is there an age limit?**
No. Students are usually between 18 and 40, and older students are very welcome.

**How big are the classes?**
A maximum of 12 students; the average is 9.

**Can I study for just one week?**
Yes, for General English and Intensive English. Other courses have a minimum length.

**Do I need to bring anything?**
Your passport, a notebook and a mobile phone. Course books are bought at the school.

**Is there Wi-Fi?**
Yes, free Wi-Fi in the whole school.

**Can I change my class if it's too easy or too difficult?**
Yes. Talk to your teacher or the academic manager, and we'll check your level.

**Can my friend and I be in the same class?**
Only if you are the same level. Being with students from other countries is a great way to practise English.

**What happens if I'm ill?**
Tell reception by phone, email or WhatsApp before 9am. Missed lessons are not refunded.

**Is London expensive?**
London can be expensive. Many students spend around £100 to £150 per week on food, travel and activities, in addition to accommodation.
