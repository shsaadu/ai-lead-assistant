# Accommodation and arrival

The school can arrange accommodation for students. All prices are per week, in British pounds (GBP).

## Homestay

- Live with a local host family or host in their home.
- Single room, breakfast and evening meal every day (half board), Wi-Fi included.
- Homes are in London travel zones 2 and 3, usually 30 to 50 minutes from the school by public transport.
- Price: £210 per week
- Students aged 16 or 17 must stay in homestay.
- Tell us about allergies, diets (for example vegetarian or halal) and pets when booking, and we'll try to find a good match.

## Student residence

- A private bedroom with its own bathroom (en-suite) in a shared flat with a kitchen.
- Self-catering: no meals included.
- 15 minutes' walk from the school.
- Bed linen, Wi-Fi, a gym and a laundry room are included.
- For students aged 18 and over only.
- Price: £320 per week
- Rooms are limited, especially from June to August, so book early.

## Arranging your own accommodation

You don't have to book accommodation with the school. Many students who stay longer rent a room privately after their first few weeks.

## Booking details

- Accommodation placement fee: £60 (one-off)
- Arrival day: Sunday. Departure day: Saturday.
- Extra nights: £35 per night in homestay, £50 per night in the student residence, if available.
- We send your accommodation details at least 7 days before you arrive.

## Airport transfers

A driver meets you at the airport and takes you to your accommodation. One-way prices:

- Heathrow: £95
- Gatwick: £105
- Stansted: £120
- London City: £75

Book transfers at least 7 days before you travel and send us your flight details.

## Getting around London

London uses travel zones. Pay for buses, the Underground (the Tube) and trains with a contactless bank card or an Oyster card. The school is in zone 1.
