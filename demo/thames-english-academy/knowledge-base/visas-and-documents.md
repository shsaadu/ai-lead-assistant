# Visas and school documents

## Do I need a visa?

Thames English Academy cannot give immigration advice. Whether you need a visa, and which type, depends on your nationality, the length of your course and your situation. UK visa rules change, so always check the official UK government website, GOV.UK, using "Check if you need a UK visa". You can also speak to a qualified immigration adviser.

## Documents the school can give you

- Enrolment letter: confirms your course, start and end dates, weekly hours and the fees you have paid. We email it within 2 working days after we receive your deposit. It is free.
- Accommodation confirmation: if you book accommodation with us, we include your accommodation address.
- Attendance letter: during your course, reception can print a letter confirming your attendance. It is free.
- Certificate: at the end of your course, if your attendance is 80% or higher.

The admissions team can help with any of these school documents. They can't help you fill in a visa application.

## If your visa is refused

If you booked a course, applied for a visa, and the visa was refused, send us a copy of the refusal letter before your course start date. We will refund all the money you paid except a £150 administration fee.

## Working in the UK

The school can't advise whether you can work in the UK. Check the rules for your visa on GOV.UK.
