# Courses and prices (2026–2027)

All prices are per week, in British pounds (GBP). One lesson is 45 minutes.

## General English

- 20 lessons per week (15 hours)
- Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 12:15pm
- All levels, from Beginner to Advanced
- Course length: 1 to 48 weeks
- Price: £230 per week

## Intensive English

- 32 lessons per week (24 hours): General English in the morning plus skills workshops (speaking, writing, pronunciation) in the afternoon
- Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 12:15pm and 1:15pm to 3:30pm
- All levels, from Elementary to Advanced
- Course length: 1 to 48 weeks
- Price: £320 per week

## IELTS Preparation

- 32 lessons per week (24 hours), focused on the IELTS Academic and General Training exams
- Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 12:15pm and 1:15pm to 3:30pm
- Minimum level: Intermediate
- Minimum course length: 4 weeks
- Includes two full practice tests with feedback
- Price: £340 per week
- The exam fee is not included. Students book the official IELTS test themselves.

## Business English

- 28 lessons per week (21 hours): meetings, presentations, emails and negotiations
- Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 12:15pm and 1:15pm to 2:45pm
- Minimum level: Upper-intermediate
- Minimum course length: 2 weeks
- Price: £360 per week

## Evening English

- 8 lessons per week (6 hours)
- Tuesday and Thursday, 6:30pm to 9:30pm
- Levels: Elementary to Upper-intermediate
- Minimum course length: 4 weeks
- Price: £110 per week
- Good for people who work or study in London during the day.

## Long-stay discounts

For General English and Intensive English only:

- 12 to 23 weeks: 5% off tuition
- 24 weeks or more: 10% off tuition

Discounts don't apply to IELTS Preparation, Business English, Evening English or accommodation.

## Other fees

- Registration fee: £75 (one-off, paid once per booking)
- Course book: £40 for every 10 weeks of study
- Airport transfers and accommodation have separate prices (see Accommodation and arrival).

## Example cost

12 weeks of General English: tuition £2,760 minus 5% (£138) = £2,622, plus £75 registration and £80 for course books = £2,777 in total, without accommodation.
