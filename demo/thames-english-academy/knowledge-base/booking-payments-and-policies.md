# Booking, payments and policies

## How to book

1. Send an enquiry (on the website chat, by email or on WhatsApp). The admissions team replies within 1 working day.
2. We send you a quote and a booking form.
3. Pay the deposit to reserve your place.
4. We email your enrolment letter and the link to the online level test.

## Payments

- Deposit: £250, paid when you book. It is part of your total fees, not an extra charge.
- The rest of the fees: due 4 weeks before your course starts. If you book less than 4 weeks before you start, pay everything when you book.
- Ways to pay: bank transfer or debit/credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express). We don't accept cash.
- All prices are in British pounds (GBP). Your bank may charge for international payments.

## Cancelling before your course starts

- More than 4 weeks before the start date: we refund everything except the £75 registration fee.
- 4 weeks or less before the start date: we refund everything except the registration fee and 1 week of tuition.
- Visa refused: see "Visas and school documents".

## After your course starts

- Tuition fees are not refundable once the course has started.
- You can change course (for example from General English to Intensive English) on any Monday, if there is space. You pay the difference in price.
- Holidays: students booked for 12 weeks or more can take 1 week of holiday for every 12 weeks of study. Your course end date moves 1 week later. Tell reception at least 2 weeks before.

## Attendance

Students should attend at least 80% of lessons. Students with attendance under 80% don't receive a course certificate.

## Insurance

We strongly recommend travel and medical insurance for your whole stay. The school can't arrange insurance.
