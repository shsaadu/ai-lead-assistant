# About Thames English Academy

Thames English Academy is an English language school in central London for adult students aged 16 and over. Students come from more than 40 countries, so classes are a real international mix.

## Location

- Address: 18 Wharf Lane, London SE1
- Nearest stations: London Bridge (6 minutes' walk) and Borough (8 minutes' walk)
- The school is a short walk from the River Thames, Borough Market and Tower Bridge.

## The school

- 14 classrooms with interactive screens
- A study centre with computers and free Wi-Fi, open until 6pm
- A student lounge with a kitchen (microwave, kettle, fridge)
- Lift access to all floors

## Classes and teachers

- Maximum 12 students per class; the average is 9.
- All teachers have a university degree and a recognised English teaching qualification (CELTA, DELTA or equivalent).
- Every student has a progress review with their teacher every 4 weeks.
- Students receive a certificate at the end of their course if their attendance is 80% or higher.

## Opening hours

- Reception: Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 6pm (UK time)
- Classes: Monday to Friday. There are no classes at weekends.
