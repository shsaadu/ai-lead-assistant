# Start dates and levels

## When can I start?

- Students who already speak some English can start any Monday (except when the school is closed).
- Complete beginners start on special beginner dates, so the whole class starts together.

## School closures

- Christmas: the school is closed from Saturday 19 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027. The last class of 2026 is on Friday 18 December, and classes start again on Monday 4 January 2027.
- There are no classes on UK bank holidays. Lessons missed because of bank holidays or the Christmas closure are not made up and are not refunded.

## Levels

The school uses five levels, linked to the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR):

| Level | CEFR | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner | A1 | You know very little English |
| Elementary | A2 | You can talk about simple, everyday things |
| Intermediate | B1 | You can manage most everyday situations |
| Upper-intermediate | B2 | You can talk about many topics with some confidence |
| Advanced | C1 | You can use English fluently for study and work |

## How is my level decided?

1. After booking, you take a free 30-minute online level test.
2. On your first morning, you have a short speaking test with a teacher.
3. You join the class that matches your level. Most students move up one level every 10 to 12 weeks of General English.

If you're not sure of your level, that's fine: the level test will find the right class for you.
