    Good to know

    Common questions.

    Can I study for just one week?
    Yes, for General English and Intensive English. Other courses have a minimum length.
    What age do I need to be?
    16 or over, and there is no upper age limit. Students aged 16 or 17 must stay in homestay.
    How is my level decided?
    After booking you take a free 30-minute online level test, then a short speaking test with a teacher on your first morning. You join the class that matches your level.
    Do I get a certificate?
    Yes, at the end of your course, if your attendance is 80% or higher.
    What happens if I'm ill?
    Tell reception by phone, email or WhatsApp before 9am. Missed lessons are not refunded.
    Is London expensive?
    It can be. Many students spend around £100 to £150 per week on food, travel and activities, in addition to accommodation.
