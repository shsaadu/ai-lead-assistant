    What runs for every enquiry

    • Acknowledge the customer within seconds, in their own language.
    • Email the owner with an English summary.
    • Hot-lead alert when the customer is ready to book, with a faster follow-up.
    • Needs-a-person alert when the assistant could not fully answer.
    • Send to the CRM, with retries and a record of every attempt.
    • Reply deadline: a reminder after 2 hours and an escalation to a manager after 4, unless someone has contacted them.
    • Follow-up after 2 days (1 day for hot leads), cancelled if the customer replies.
    • Booking: a confirmation in the customer's language, reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before, a "we missed you" message after a no-show, and the old reminders cancelled if they move or cancel.
    • Invoices: sent in the customer's language with a payment link, then a heads-up 3 days before, a reminder on the due date, a firmer one at 7 days late, a final notice at 14 days that also asks the owner to call. It all stops the moment the invoice is paid, and part payments reduce what is asked for.
    • Weekly summary every Monday at 08:00, and a daily health check that warns about a quiet week or leads stuck outside the CRM.

    How the retry rule works

    A message is sent straight away. If the CRM is temporarily unavailable, it tries again after 1 minute, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours and 6 hours. If it still fails, the run is marked dead and you are alerted. If the CRM says the address is gone for good (an error such as 410), it stops straight away, because trying again can't help. Each message carries a unique ID, so if one is delivered twice by accident the CRM ignores the repeat.
