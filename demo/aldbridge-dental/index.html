Emergencies

In pain? We keep same-day appointments free.

Toothache, a broken tooth, a lost filling or swelling: we keep emergency slots free every weekday morning.

Call 999 or go to A&E if swelling affects your breathing, swallowing or vision, or bleeding won't stop.

  1. Weekdays: phone at 8am on 020 7946 0321 for a same-day appointment.
  2. Private emergency visit: £95, including an X-ray and pain relief treatment.
  3. When we're closed: call NHS 111 for urgent dental care.
