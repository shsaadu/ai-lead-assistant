# About Aldbridge Dental

Aldbridge Dental is a family and cosmetic dental practice in East London. We see adults and children, offer both NHS and private treatment, and specialise in Invisalign, dental implants and teeth whitening.

## Address and getting here

- Address: 41 Market Row, London E1 (fictional demo address)
- Nearest stations: Aldgate East and Whitechapel, both about 5 minutes' walk
- Buses: 25, 205 and 254 stop outside
- The practice is on the ground floor, with step-free access and an accessible toilet.
- There is no patient car park. Pay-and-display parking is available on nearby streets.

## Languages

As well as English, our team speaks Bengali, Hindi, Urdu, Arabic and French. Tell us when you book if you'd like a dentist or nurse who speaks your language.

## Nervous patients

Many of our patients feel anxious about the dentist. Tell us when you book and we'll give you a longer first appointment, explain everything before we start, and agree a signal you can use to stop at any time. Sedation is available for some treatments (see the treatments and prices information).
