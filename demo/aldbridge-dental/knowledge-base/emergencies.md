# Dental emergencies

## Same-day emergency appointments

We keep emergency appointments free every weekday morning for patients in pain.

- Phone at 8am for a same-day emergency appointment (Monday to Friday).
- Private emergency appointment: £95 (includes examination, X-ray and pain relief treatment).
- NHS patients pay the urgent treatment charge.
- On Saturdays we see private emergencies when slots are free.

Common reasons to book an emergency appointment: toothache, a broken or chipped tooth, a lost filling or crown, swelling, or a knocked-out tooth.

## Knocked-out adult tooth

Pick it up by the top (crown), not the root. If it's clean, gently put it back in its socket; if not, keep it in milk. See a dentist within 1 hour.

## When we're closed

- For urgent dental problems outside our opening hours, call NHS 111. They can arrange urgent dental care.
- Go to A&E or call 999 if you have: swelling that affects your breathing, swallowing or vision; heavy bleeding that won't stop; or a serious injury to your face or mouth.
