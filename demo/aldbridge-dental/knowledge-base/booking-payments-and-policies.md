# Booking, payments and policies

## How to book

- Phone 020 7946 0321, message us on WhatsApp on 07700 900321, or leave your details with the website assistant.
- Reception confirms every appointment by text message, and sends a reminder 2 days before.

## Cancellations and missed appointments

- Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or move an appointment.
- Private patients who miss an appointment or cancel with less than 24 hours' notice may be charged £30 for a check-up or hygiene visit and £50 for longer treatment appointments.
- NHS patients who miss two appointments in a row may be asked to rejoin the waiting list.

## Payment

- We accept card, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We don't accept cheques.
- Payment is taken on the day for each appointment.
- For treatments over £1,000, you pay a deposit of 20% when you book and the rest across your appointments.

## Interest-free finance

- 0% finance is available for private treatment over £1,000, including Invisalign, implants and veneers.
- Spread the cost over 6 to 12 months interest-free, or up to 36 months with interest.
- Finance is subject to status and a credit check by our finance provider. Ask reception for details.

## Dental plan

- The Aldbridge Care Plan costs £16.50 a month and includes two check-ups and two hygiene visits a year, plus 10% off private treatment.

## Children

- Children under 16 must come with a parent or guardian.
