# NHS treatment and new patients

## Are you taking new NHS patients?

Yes, but NHS places for adults are limited. We keep a waiting list, and the current wait for a new adult NHS check-up is around 6 to 8 weeks. Children under 18 can join as NHS patients without waiting.

Private new patient appointments are usually available within a week.

## NHS charges (England)

NHS dental charges are set by the government. The charges for 2025 to 2026 are:

- Band 1 (examination, diagnosis, X-rays, scale and polish if needed): £27.50
- Band 2 (everything in Band 1, plus fillings, root canal treatment or extractions): £75.30
- Band 3 (everything in Bands 1 and 2, plus crowns, dentures or bridges): £326.70
- Urgent NHS treatment: £27.50

The government can change these charges each April; the current amounts are on nhs.uk.

NHS treatment is free for under-18s, under-19s in full-time education, pregnant women and those who have had a baby in the last 12 months, and people receiving certain benefits. You'll be asked to show proof.

## Your first visit

- Please arrive 10 minutes early to fill in a short medical history form.
- Bring a list of any medicines you take.
- Your first appointment lasts about 30 minutes, and longer for nervous patients.
- We register patients of all ages, including whole families.

## Joining the practice

To book, phone us, message us on WhatsApp, or leave your details with the assistant on our website and reception will contact you within 1 working day to arrange a time.
