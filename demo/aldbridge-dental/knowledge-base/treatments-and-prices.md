# Private treatments and prices

These are our private prices. NHS patients pay the NHS bands instead (see the NHS information). Prices are from-prices: your dentist confirms the exact cost in a written treatment plan before any treatment starts.

## Check-ups and hygiene

- New patient examination (including X-rays if needed): £75
- Routine check-up (existing patients): £50
- Child check-up (under 18): £30
- Scale and polish with the hygienist (30 minutes): £70
- Airflow stain removal: £90

## Fillings and general treatment

- White (tooth-coloured) filling: from £120
- Root canal treatment: front tooth from £350, back tooth from £550
- Simple extraction: from £150
- Surgical extraction (including wisdom teeth): from £300

## Crowns and bridges

- Porcelain crown: from £650
- Bridge: from £1,300

## Cosmetic dentistry

- Professional teeth whitening (home kit with custom trays): £295
- Composite bonding: from £250 per tooth
- Porcelain veneers: from £750 per tooth

## Invisalign (clear aligners)

- Free Invisalign consultation, including a 3D scan and a preview of your new smile
- Invisalign Lite (mild cases): from £2,500
- Invisalign Comprehensive: from £3,800
- Treatment usually takes 6 to 18 months.
- Includes retainers at the end of treatment.

## Dental implants

- Implant consultation with Dr Hughes: £95, taken off the cost of treatment if you go ahead
- Single implant with crown: from £2,400
- Implant-retained dentures: from £5,500

## Sedation

- Intravenous (IV) sedation for nervous patients: £300 per appointment, for suitable adults only, assessed by the dentist first.
