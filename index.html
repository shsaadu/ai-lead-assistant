Questions

Good questions to ask.

Will it make things up?

It's instructed to answer only from the information you provide. When something isn't covered, it says so and offers to pass the question to your team. You can read every conversation in your dashboard.

What does my team need to do?

Send us your information and add one line of code to your website, or ask whoever manages it to. We do the setup, testing and updates.

Which languages does it speak?

It replies in the language the customer writes in. The chat window and enquiry form are translated into 12 languages, including Arabic (right to left), Chinese, Spanish, Turkish, Japanese and Russian.

Will it work with my website?

It works on most websites and site builders that let you add a line of code. If yours doesn't, we'll find a way together.

What about data protection?

We handle customer data under UK data protection law. The chat shows customers a notice that your team may read the conversation, and we delete a customer's data on request. See our privacy policy for details.

How does pricing work?

A one-off setup fee and a monthly plan. Plans roll monthly, with no long contract: you can cancel with 30 days' notice. We give you a fixed quote before any work starts, and our first 10 clients get half-price setup. See the pricing page for details.

Can I see it working first?

Yes. The demos on this site show each service working, and on a free call we will talk through how it would work for your business. We build and test your assistant on your own information before it goes live.

Can it answer on WhatsApp?

Not yet. It's in development. Customers can already leave a WhatsApp number instead of an email in the enquiry form.
