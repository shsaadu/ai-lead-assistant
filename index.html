What it does

More than a chatbot. A front desk.

Answers from your information

Only uses what you give it. If something isn't covered, it says so, instead of guessing prices, dates or availability.

Speaks their language

Replies in the language your customer writes in. The chat window and forms switch too, including right-to-left Arabic.

Knows when to hand over

Spots customers who are ready to book or want a person, and asks for their details at the right moment, not on every message.

Your questions, your form

Collects exactly what you need to quote: dates, budget, service, phone or WhatsApp. Customers can skip email if they prefer.

Briefs your team

Every enquiry arrives by email and in a dashboard, with a one-line English summary and the customer's language.

Safe by design

Guardrails for topics it shouldn't advise on, spam and abuse protection, and a data processing agreement with every client.

Questions

Good questions to ask.

Will it make things up?

It's instructed to answer only from the information you provide. When something isn't covered, it says so and offers to pass the question to your team. You can read every conversation in your dashboard.

What does my team need to do?

Send us your information and add one line of code to your website, or ask whoever manages it to. We do the setup, testing and updates.

Which languages does it speak?

It replies in the language the customer writes in. The chat window and enquiry form are translated into 12 languages, including Arabic (right to left), Chinese, Spanish, Turkish, Japanese and Russian.

Will it work with my website?

It works on most websites and site builders that let you add a line of code. If yours doesn't, we'll find a way together.

What about data protection?

We sign a data processing agreement with every client, the chat shows customers a notice that your team may read it, and we delete a customer's data on request. See our privacy policy for details.

What happens after the free pilot?

You see every conversation and enquiry it handled. If it's worth it, choose a plan. If not, we remove it, with no charge and no awkward conversation.

Can it answer on WhatsApp?

Not yet. It's in development. Customers can already leave a WhatsApp number instead of an email in the enquiry form.
